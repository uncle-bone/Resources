# Changelog

## 1.1.3

- New optional Well Fed reminder: shows "WELL FED MISSING" at the top of the screen when your food buff is gone. Dismiss it until you eat again.
- Some minor upgrades and bugfixes.

## 1.1.2

- Health bar now shows incoming heals.
- Fixed Mark of the Wild / Thorns timers turning red with no time in combat.
- Fixed Mark of the Wild timer after recasting it in combat.
- Some minor upgrades and bugfixes.

## 1.1.1

- Clearcasting icon can now be moved anywhere on the screen (unlock and drag it).
- Fixed the mana cost on the mana bar disappearing when pressing a spell again mid-cast.
- Fixed the Clearcasting timer not restarting when it procs again right away.
- Fixed Mark of the Wild / Thorns icons not showing after reloading in combat.
- Some minor upgrades and bugfixes.

## 1.1.0

- New cooldown layout editor: rows of slots, bigger icons, hidden tray, spacing and position options. Each form has its own icons, the layout shape is shared.
- Custom tracking: add any spell or item (trinkets, potions) to track its cooldown.
- Mark of the Wild and Thorns icons with timers, click to cast on yourself.
- Optional health bar and bar order.
- Mana bar shows the cost of the spell you're casting.
- Added Shifting Power, removed Tiger's Fury.
- Options window in Blizzard style, minimap button always shown.
- Now All Rights Reserved.
- Some minor upgrades and bugfixes.

## 1.0.0

- First release.
- Form-aware rage / energy bar, mana bar with the exact value, and a combo points bar
  in Cat Form with a 5th combo point highlight.
- Clearcasting alert with a countdown above the bars when Omen of Clarity procs.
- Cooldown tracking for Cat, Bear and caster abilities, with a red "active" effect for
  buffs, a shared cooldown for both Feral Charges, and a blue tint when you don't have
  enough power.
- Separate icon layouts for Cat, Bear and caster form; any ability can be shown in any
  form.
- Racial abilities: Elune's Light and War Stomp.
